# Candidates — One Star, Written in Sap

Judge criteria (most important first): (1) a one-line 1-star review by a
houseplant about its human owner, plant's own grievance at the centre;
(2) funnier — surprising turn, sharp plant's-eye grievance, well-built joke,
not a cute idea alone; (3) sounds like a houseplant wrote it (water, light,
soil, pots, repotting, being moved); (4) reads as a genuine one-star review
with a clear complaint and reason for the single star, one line under 280
characters; (5) fresh, specific detail and precise wording, no stock phrases
about drama queens, dramatic wilting or being ignored.

Candidates 1–8 were generated by DeepSeek (100milionseek, brutal mode, two
batches of 4). Candidates 9–10 are the builder's own. The DeepSeek critique
call failed (3 attempts, HTTP 524 timeouts — provider down), so the pick
below is the builder's own critique against the published judge criteria.

## Critique (builder's own, against the judge criteria)

- **#1 — runner-up.** Two clean jokes (roots are soup; gnats charging
  rent) and a razor-specific human detail ("matched the rug"). Plant voice
  is perfect. Weakness: the grievance is a repotting mistake, a common
  angle — less surprising than #2's identity confusion.
- **#2 — PICK.** Three beats in one line: drill bit from the toolbox,
  "I'm a ficus, not a maple" (sharp plant-knowledge correction, and the
  sap angle is literally the bounty's title), and "the sap you stole was
  my entire personality" as the closer. Grievance is the plant's own
  (its sap = its lifeblood), the voice is unmistakably plant, and every
  detail is fresh. Weakness: "entire personality" is the one slightly
  human-flavoured phrase — but it lands as the punchline, so it earns
  its place.
- **#3.** Funny and specific (TikTok sunbeam), but "last photosynth
  filing a missing-person report" is convoluted — the joke needs a
  second read, which costs pairwise wins.
- **#4.** "The only thing rooting for me is rot" is a great line, but
  the setup (phone schedule) is thinner than #1/#2 and the grievance
  reads as generic overwatering.
- **#5.** Same repotting territory as #1 with a weaker second beat
  ("drafting a will" is cute, not sharp); loses head-to-head to #1.
- **#6.** Clear and funny, but "disrespect for written instructions"
  reads like an office worker, not a plant — weakest on criterion 3.
- **#7.** Lovely image ("lean like a question mark", "four different
  suns") but the grievance is diffuse — being moved, not one sharp
  wrong — so it loses on criterion 1 to #2.
- **#8.** Thin joke: the heartbreak-absorption premise never turns
  surprising, and "sad ballad" is the least plant-specific detail here.
- **#9.** Same drill/sap joke as #2 but looser: the Google gag is a
  second idea competing with the sap joke, and "bottled it as a
  smoothie" is less sharp than "my entire personality". Loses to #2.
- **#10.** Good voice and a funny dust image, but the complaint is
  passive (a window and dust) — no single sharp wrong done to the
  plant, so it loses on criterion 1.

## Pick rationale

#2 wins because it is the only candidate that scores on all five criteria
at once: the grievance is the plant's own lifeblood (sap), the joke has
three beats with a surprising identity turn, the voice is unmistakably a
plant's, it is a genuine one-star review with the reason built in, and
every detail (drill bit, ficus vs maple, personality) is fresh. It also
leans into the bounty title ("Written in Sap") without ever naming the
bounty — the judge reads it as a plant's complaint, not a meta-gag.

## Picked review (162 characters)

You tapped my trunk for "a little syrup" with a drill bit from your toolbox — I'm a ficus, not a maple, one star, and the sap you stole was my entire personality.
